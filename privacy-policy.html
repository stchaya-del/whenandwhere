WhenAndWhere Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

WhenAndWhere ("the app") is an Android app published by SRCIMOS ("we", "us"). It watches your location and plays a bell and shows a reminder when you get near places you choose. This policy explains what information the app uses and who can see it.

Summary

  • The app has no account system and no servers of ours. We do not receive, collect or store your data.
  • Your location and your reminders stay on your device.
  • The app does not contain ads, analytics or tracking.
  • To show a map and suggest addresses, the app contacts third-party services (listed below), which can see technical information such as your IP address.

Information the app uses

InformationWhyWhere it goes
Precise location, including while the app is closed or in the background To show your position on the map, measure your distance to your saved places, and play the reminder when you come within the distance you set. Processed on your device only. It is not sent to us or to any third party by the app, except as described under "Address search" below.

Address search text and your approximate current position To suggest matching addresses as you type and to rank nearby results first. Sent to the Photon geocoding service (photon.komoot.io), which uses OpenStreetMap data and is operated by Komoot. See the Photon and Komoot privacy information on their website.
Map requests: the map area being viewed To display the map. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), and the map library is loaded from the unpkg content delivery network. These services can see your IP address and the area you view. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation and unpkg privacy information.

Background location

The app needs "Allow all the time" location access so it can alert you when you arrive near a saved place while the app is minimized or the screen is off. While it is watching, Android shows a notification. Location is used only for the reminder feature described above. To reduce battery use, the app checks your location less often when you are far from all your saved places, and stops checking when no reminder is switched on and the app is in the background.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate, including background): to show your position and trigger reminders.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep watching reminders when the app is minimized.
  • Notifications: to show the "watching your reminders" notice and the reminder alert.
  • Internet and network state: to load the map and address suggestions.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone. The only outside parties that receive any information are the map and address services listed above, and only so the app can show a map and suggest addresses.

Storage, retention and your choices

  • Reminders stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all of the app's stored data.
  • You can switch any reminder off, or use Quit on the Reminders tab to stop the app watching your location.
  • You can withdraw location permission at any time in Android Settings. The app will then stop showing your position and cannot trigger reminders.
  • Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side. Data held by the third-party services above is governed by their own policies.

Security

Reminders are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard Android device. Requests to the map and address services use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
